# Open work — the handoff a fresh session reads

Only genuinely OPEN work belongs here. Anything solved, fixed, cracked or superseded moves to
`archive/`. Before adding a section, ask whether a future session must ACT on it. A finding belongs
in `worker_src/core.md` as a rule, or nowhere.

Record every address you are hand-working (any `.cpp` under `pad/` or `retry_work/`) here while you
work on it; `selfcheck.py` fails while one is missing.

    OPEN_RESIDUES.md      the catalogue of named residues nobody has cracked (read on demand)
    INVENTORY.md          what every script is for
    worker_src/core.md    the rules workers actually read
